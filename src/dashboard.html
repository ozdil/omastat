󰒃 Zero-PII Cryptographic Privacy Standard

OmaStat is purpose-built to eliminate user tracking, telemetry backlash, and surveillance vectors.

  • Zero User Identifiers: No usernames, emails, hostnames, or machine-ids are ever transmitted.
  • Zero Device Fingerprinting: No MAC addresses or hardware serial numbers are collected.
  • Hardware Archetypes: Instead of unique IDs, machines submit aggregate build signatures (e.g. OMA-U7-4060-31G-240H).
  • Pure Histogram Tables: Cloudflare D1 stores only aggregate bucket counters. Raw submission logs are discarded at the edge.
  • Enforced 16 KiB Ceiling: Strictly guarded against denial-of-service or payload injection attacks.

Both OmaStat and OmaRank are 100% open source under the MIT license and open for public verification.
